## Key Findings from Initial Analysis

### Five Keystone Properties
1. **Predictability:** Evidence that quantum technology will surpass classical
2. **Typicality:** Advantage holds for average-case, not just worst-case
3. **Robustness:** Persists under noise and hardware imperfections
4. **Verifiability:** Results can be independently validated
5. **Usefulness:** Solves problems users actually care about

### Four Realms
1. Computational (BPP ≠ BQP assumption)
2. Learning/Sensing (physical law foundation)
3. Cryptographic/Communication (physical security)
4. Space advantages (quantum compression)

### Theorem 1 (Most Novel)
Decision problem "does circuit C beat Pauli propagation?" is in BQP but not BPP
(assuming BPP ≠ BQP). Implication: some quantum advantages are classically
undetectable.

### Honesty of the Paper
- Admits BPP ≠ BQP is unproven (30+ years)
- Acknowledges Tang's dequantization killed quantum recommendation systems
- States LIGO is the ONLY real-world entanglement-enhanced sensing success
- HNLS criterion shows asymptotic sensing advantage is "fundamentally unattainable"
  in generic noise scenarios
- Admits QM has never been tested at the complexity frontier
