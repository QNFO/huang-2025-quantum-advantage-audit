## Overview

Deep research audit and critical engagement with:
> Huang, H.-Y., Choi, S., McClean, J. R., & Preskill, J. (2025).
> *The vast world of quantum advantage.* arXiv:2508.05720.

This project conducts a full-spectrum analysis of the Huang et al. framework for quantum advantage, its relationship to QNFO's existing research corpus, and the intellectual tensions between the quantum computing establishment's most honest self-assessment and QNFO's skeptical posture.
