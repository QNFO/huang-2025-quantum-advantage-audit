## For Huang et al. (2025) Claims

### [CHECK: 2028] Five-Keystone Framework Citation Impact
By 2028, "The vast world of quantum advantage" will have accumulated ≥200 citations and the five-keystone framework will be referenced as a standard taxonomy in at least 5 independent review/perspective papers.
**Status:** PENDING
**Disconfirmation:** <100 citations or no adoption of framework terminology by 2028.
**Rationale:** The paper has strong author pedigree (Preskill) and fills a genuine gap (no systematic framework for quantum advantage). But citation count depends on community reception, which is uncertain.

### [CHECK: 2030] Theorem 1 Generalization
By 2030, at least one paper will have proven (or disproven) that Theorem 1 generalizes beyond Pauli propagation to broader classes of classical simulation methods.
**Status:** PENDING
**Disconfirmation:** No generalization paper by 2030, or generalization is disproven.
**Rationale:** Meta-complexity is a young field. Generalization is non-trivial. The theorem may remain a narrow result.

### [CHECK: 2030] BPP ≠ BQP Still Unproven
By 2030, the BPP vs. BQP question will remain unresolved and the quantum computing field will have shifted its primary justification from computational speedup to sensing/communication advantages.
**Status:** PENDING
**Disconfirmation:** BPP ≠ BQP is proven (or disproven) by 2030, OR the field maintains computational speedup as primary justification despite continued lack of proof.
**Rationale:** 30 years of no progress on this question suggests structural difficulty. The field's framing shift from "supremacy" to "utility" already shows this trend.

### [CHECK: 2027] Next Major Dequantization
By 2027, at least one more widely-publicized "quantum advantage" claim will be dequantized (classical algorithm shown to match or within polynomial factor).
**Status:** PENDING
**Disconfirmation:** No new dequantization of a major claim by 2027.
**Rationale:** The trend is clear (Tang 2019, 2021; Aharonov 2023; Begušić 2024, 2025; Fontana 2025). It would be surprising if this stops.
