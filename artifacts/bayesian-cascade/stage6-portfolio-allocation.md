## Computational Investment Portfolio

Based on the Bayesian cascade analysis, here is the recommended allocation of intellectual and financial capital across post-classical computing approaches:

| Substrate/Paradigm | Allocation | Justification |
|:-------------------|:-----------|:--------------|
| **Quantum Sensing & Metrology** | 30% | Only realm with demonstrated, commercially deployed quantum advantage (LIGO, NV centers, atomic clocks). Physical-law foundation. Constant-factor improvements are real and valuable. |
| **Classical Algorithm Development** | 20% | The most consistent source of progress. Every major "quantum advantage" claim eventually faces classical competition. Investing here is a hedge against all quantum paradigms. |
| **Analog Quantum Simulation** | 15% | More mature than FTQC; doesn't require full error correction. Directly addresses the strongest use case (materials simulation). |
| **Thermodynamic/Optical Computing** | 12% | QNFO's favored alternatives. Promising but unproven at commercial scale. Deserves exploration proportional to risk. |
| **Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (FTQC)** | 10% | Revised upward from QNFO's 5%. Theorem 1 suggests classical analysis may have blind spots. A minimal allocation hedges against being wrong. |
| **Neuromorphic Computing** | 5% | Niche applications in pattern recognition and low-power inference. |
| **Foundational Research** | 5% | BPP vs. BQP resolution, meta-complexity, quantum foundations. The uncertainty is fundamental — reducing it has value. |
| **Reversible Classical Computing** | 3% | Theoretical elegance but no commercial path. Minimal allocation. |

## Anti-Fragility Floor

**Minimum allocation to hedge pessimistic paths:**

| Scenario | Hedge |
|:---------|:------|
| BPP = BQP (all computational advantage collapses) | Quantum sensing (30%) + classical algorithms (20%) = 50% protected |
| FTQC succeeds spectacularly | FTQC allocation (10%) captures some upside |
| All quantum technologies fail | Classical algorithms (20%) + foundational research (5%) maintain research capability |
| Theorem 1 generalizes (quantum needed for discovery) | FTQC (10%) + sensing (30%) provide quantum capabilities |
