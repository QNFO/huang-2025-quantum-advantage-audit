# Stage 1: Paradigm-Shift Candidate Identification

**Date:** 2026-07-19

---

## Candidate 1: Theorem 1 as Gateway — "Quantum Advantage Requires Quantum Discovery"

**Thesis:** The Huang et al. Theorem 1 implies that the full landscape of quantum advantage is classically undetectable. This shifts the paradigm from "prove quantum advantage on paper" to "discover quantum advantage experimentally."

**Probability:** 0.25 (conditional on BPP ≠ BQP, which is itself uncertain)
**Impact:** Very High — would fundamentally change how quantum advantage research is conducted and funded
**Timeline:** 5-10 years for paradigm to take hold if Theorem 1 is validated and generalized
**Testability:** Theorem 1 is already proven (conditional). The open question is whether it generalizes beyond Pauli propagation.
**Dependency chain:** BPP ≠ BQP → Theorem 1 accepted → generalization proven → paradigm shift in research methodology

---

## Candidate 2: Economic Collapse of Gate-Model QC — "The $35B Bet Fails"

**Thesis:** Fault-tolerant quantum computation will not deliver commercially useful results within the investment horizon, leading to a collapse in funding and consolidation around sensing/communication applications.

**Probability:** 0.35 (QNFO estimate) to 0.15 (industry estimate)
**Impact:** Very High — restructuring of quantum technology investment landscape
**Timeline:** 2030-2035 (current roadmaps extend to ~2033)
**Testability:** Falsifiable by any commercially useful FTQC computation at lower joules/solution than classical
**Dependency chain:** QEC overhead remains high → no commercially useful FTQC → funding dries up → consolidation

---

## Candidate 3: Classical Algorithms Keep Winning — "The Shrinking Window"

**Thesis:** Continued advances in classical algorithms (tensor networks, Pauli propagation, ML-based methods) will shrink the space of potential quantum advantage to near-zero for practical problem sizes.

**Probability:** 0.30
**Impact:** High — would make most quantum computing investment retroactively unjustified
**Timeline:** Ongoing (Aharonov 2023, Begušić 2024/2025, Fontana 2025 demonstrate trend)
**Testability:** Track the set of problems where quantum advantage is claimed vs. subsequently dequantized
**Dependency chain:** Classical algorithm innovation rate > QC hardware improvement rate → window closes

---

## Candidate 4: Post-Classical Diversification — "QNFO's Portfolio Wins"

**Thesis:** A diversified portfolio approach to post-classical computing (thermodynamic, optical, neuromorphic, analog quantum simulation, limited FTQC) will outperform concentrated FTQC investment in terms of commercially useful results.

**Probability:** 0.20
**Impact:** Very High — would validate QNFO's Problem-Substrate Mapping framework
**Timeline:** 7-15 years for full portfolio evaluation
**Testability:** Track commercial results from each substrate; compare to FTQC-only portfolio
**Dependency chain:** FTQC fails or underperforms → alternative substrates mature → diversified portfolio wins

---

## Candidate 5: Synthesis — "Huang-QNFO Reconciliation"

**Thesis:** The Huang et al. framework and QNFO's critique converge into a unified honest framework for computational investment that incorporates (a) the five keystone properties, (b) joules per solution as the sixth keystone, (c) a diversified portfolio approach, and (d) symmetric falsifiability standards for all paradigms.

**Probability:** 0.15
**Impact:** High — would be the first intellectually honest synthesis of quantum computing optimism and skepticism
**Timeline:** 3-5 years if engagement happens
**Testability:** Publication of joint framework or parallel publications that cite each other
**Dependency chain:** QNFO acknowledges its own failures → Huang et al. incorporate economic dimension → synthesis emerges
