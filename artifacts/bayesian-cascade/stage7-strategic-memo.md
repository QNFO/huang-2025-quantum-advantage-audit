# Stage 7: Strategic Memo

**To:** QNFO Research Collective
**Re:** Engagement Strategy for Huang et al. (2025) "The vast world of quantum advantage"

---

## Executive Summary

Huang, Choi, McClean & Preskill (2025) have produced the most intellectually honest document about quantum advantage to emerge from the quantum computing establishment. Their five-keystone framework (Predictability, Typicality, Robustness, Verifiability, Usefulness) is a genuine contribution that largely converges with QNFO's own values. Their Theorem 1 — proving that detecting quantum advantage is classically hard under BPP ≠ BQP — is the most sophisticated challenge to QNFO's epistemic posture yet produced. However, the paper omits the economic cost dimension entirely and does not engage with QNFO's ontological critique.

**QNFO's strongest response:** Theorem 1, if correct, strengthens the case for diversified post-classical investment, not concentrated FTQC investment. Unpredictability justifies broader exploration.

**QNFO's most urgent vulnerability:** The "Qubit Delusion" ontological critique proves too much — if every useful abstraction is an "epistemic failure," all of science is guilty. QNFO must either refine this argument or abandon it as its central claim.

**Recommended immediate actions:** (1) Recover the missing "Shor's Assumptions" paper body — this is the central gap in QNFO's intellectual architecture. (2) Publish symmetric falsification conditions for QNFO's own claims. (3) Compute the quantitative joules-per-solution threshold for FTQC viability.

---

## Key Findings

### Convergence with QNFO

1. Both frameworks identify pseudo-advantages as the central risk.
2. Both demand verifiability and usefulness as non-negotiable criteria.
3. Both acknowledge that BPP ≠ BQP is unproven.
4. Both recognize classical algorithm advancement as relentless competition.
5. Both seek honest frameworks for evaluating technological claims.

### Divergence from QNFO

1. Huang et al. accept the qubit-gate-circuit paradigm; QNFO challenges it at the ontological level.
2. Huang et al. omit cost from their framework; QNFO centers it.
3. Huang et al. are cautiously optimistic; QNFO is structurally skeptical.
4. Huang et al. do not address investment allocation; QNFO proposes specific allocations.

### Key Challenge: Theorem 1

The theorem proves that some quantum advantages are classically undetectable. If correct, this challenges QNFO's implicit claim that classical analysis can reliably map the quantum advantage landscape. However, the theorem is conditional on BPP ≠ BQP and specific to Pauli propagation detection. Generalization is not proved.
